# Clash Of Clans Tracker

### A project made by Ashwath Srinivasan that *IS NOT* vibe coded, as a passion project

---

## About Clash of Clans :

> It is a game of strategically upgrading your base and progressing.
>
> As the name implies, you along with your clan work together on different goals and have wars with opposing clans
>
> Clash of clans is a game i have played from when i was a child. 
>
> It is one such game that is very close to my heart.

---

## Why this project?

> There are multiple ways to aproach the progression of your village, you can upgrade your buildings in your preffered order
>
> This project aims to help users see their progress and how much of their journey is left
>
> It also suggests *Personalised and optimal* ways to progress your journey.
